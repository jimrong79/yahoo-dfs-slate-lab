Net profit = payouts − recorded entry fees. ROI = net profit ÷ entry fees. Cash rate = entries with a positive cash payout ÷ entries. A payout does not necessarily mean that entry made a profit.

Missing dates mean no imported records, not verified zero activity. Platform exports can end on different dates. Entry fees may include promotional or ticket-funded entries. DraftKings ticket prizes are tracked separately from cash payouts; Yahoo does not distinguish ticket value in this export. External data and subscription expenses are excluded.

Contest types describe payout formats. “50/50” here is not a projection-mode label. The exports do not identify the model used, and entries from the same slate can be correlated. These results do not establish a lasting advantage or predict future returns.
